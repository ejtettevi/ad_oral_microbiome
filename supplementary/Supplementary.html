Fig. 2: Order-level data (C) highlight the significant enrichment of Pseudomonadales and Flavobacteriales alongside an upward trend in Staphylococcales and Saccharimonadales in AD cases, as determined by ANCOM-BC2. At the family level (D), Thioglobaceae is significantly enriched in cases, while Tannerellaceae, Actinomycetaceae, and Selenomonadaceae are significantly depleted. Genus-level profiling (E) identifies Streptococcus as the most abundant core genus in both cohorts; however, ANCOM-BC2 reveals the highly significant enrichment of Gemella, SUP05 cluster, TM7x, and Fusobacterium, alongside significant depletion of F0332 and Selenomonas. Collectively, these high-resolution differential abundance analyses delineate a highly robust dysbiotic signature in AD cases.
